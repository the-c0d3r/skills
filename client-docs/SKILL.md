---
name: client-docs
description: Use when writing or reviewing client-facing documents — threat models, design docs, slide decks, reports, or summaries intended for external audiences.
---

Use neutral, factual language. State limitations as scope boundaries or design constraints — not as negative properties or someone else's problem.

Avoid: "blindly", "client's responsibility", "not preventable"
Prefer: "outside scope", "not within TOE", "detectable but not in scope to prevent"
