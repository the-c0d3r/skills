---
name: presentations
description: Use when writing, reviewing, or editing slide decks or presentations.
---

- Keep bullets short — break long lines into sub-bullets or cut what isn't essential.
- Cut content that can be explained verbally; slides don't need to be self-contained.
- Heading-only slides as section dividers are intentional; don't flag them as errors.
- Index/agenda slides must match what's actually in the deck.
- Use tables for structured comparisons, prose for everything else.
- Open questions should be positive-leading (what's working before what's broken).
- Title slide: title is the product or subject name, subtitle is the document type; no tagline.
- Slide titles: a short noun naming the topic, not a sentence describing a process or status.
- Table headers: one or two words; put any definition a term needs in a footnote.
- Merge sub-groups the audience doesn't need separated under one heading, and related items into one bullet.
- Cut qualifiers that aren't the slide's point (background facts, deadlines, "required"); keep a specific fact when it supports the argument.
- Use the customer-facing term, and the same term for the same idea across columns.
- Range labels: full start and end, e.g. "May 27 – Sep 27", not "May–Sep 27".
- Notes to self (todos, reminders) go in source comments, never on a slide.
