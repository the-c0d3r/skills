# Guiding questions

Pick 3-5 per section that matter for this product. Skip questions the user or the provided files already answer.

## Target Market
- Who signs the purchase order, and who uses the product day to day?
- Which segments are there, and what does each require that the others don't (certification, compliance, integrations)?
- Which segment comes first, and is there a named first customer?
- What makes a prospect a good fit: how close must they be to the first customer to need no adaptation?
- How big is the market, and is that figure needed for a decision, or only for context? (If only context, it belongs in research.)
- Which segments fit the product's strengths best, and which only partly (e.g. a web-only product vs sectors that require offline use)?
- What single criterion separates the segments, and where do borderline organisations fall?

## Competitive Position
- Who is the buyer most likely to compare against? Is there a local or incumbent competitor that matters more than the global leaders?
- What are the one or two reasons a buyer picks this product? Can a competitor copy them easily?
- Where do competitors clearly lead (certification, performance, breadth, price, brand)? Does that block any target segment?
- Does customisation help win deals, or does it undermine staying a product?

## Product Model
- What does every customer get, regardless of tier?
- What is sold separately, and why: cost to build, cost to support, or price discrimination?
- What will you refuse to customise? What customer-specific requests are configuration, and which are paid services?
- What are the headline numbers a buyer will ask for (capacity, speed, users), and are they tiers?

## Business Model
- One-time purchase, licence, or subscription? What recurs?
- What is priced by tier, and what by add-on?
- Is support and maintenance sold separately or bundled? For how long?
- Does the revenue model match what the company wants from the product (see Strategic Fit)?
- Is there a public price point from a competitor to anchor against?

## Hardware and IP (optional)
- What hardware is custom-built, and what is off-the-shelf?
- Where does the proprietary value sit: hardware, software, or integration?
- What supply or component constraints exist (origin, lead time, export restrictions)?

## Certification Strategy (optional)
- Which buyer requires which certification? Is it a hard requirement or a tender advantage?
- What is in scope of the certification, and what is left out?
- What does it cost, how long does it take, and who pays?
- In what order: national first, international later, or both together?
- When does work need to start relative to development?

## Go-to-Market
- Direct sales, resellers or system integrators, or OEM through a larger vendor? Which fits the team size?
- Who closes the first customer, and does the same approach close the next ones?
- Can the first customer introduce others?
- Government buyers: which procurement route (quotation, tender, limited tender, existing contract) and value threshold applies? Is a standalone pilot possible below the threshold? When does the budget year end? Is certification required before the PO or deployment?

## Regulatory Exposure (optional)
- Is the product subject to export control? Which category, and is a permit needed per shipment?
- Are there components whose origin brings another country's export rules into play?
- What must be in place before the first international sale?

## Strategic Fit
- Is this a product line worth sustained investment, or a project for one customer?
- What does the company want from it: recurring revenue, a reference customer, capability?
- What investment does that justify, and what must be proven first?

## Timeline
- What is the goal date (e.g. first PO), and what must exist before the customer can act on it? Plan backward from it.
- Which tracks run in parallel (commercial, development, certification), and which milestone depends on which?
- What is the first hard date (customer PO, pilot, tender deadline)?
- Which milestones depend on outside parties (customer, certification body, supplier)?
- When does development finish, and does every section of the plan agree on that date?
